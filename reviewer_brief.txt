PROVERBGAP PROJECT BRIEF FOR REVIEWER AGENTS
================================================================================

1. WHAT IS PROVERBGAP?
ProverbGap is a multilingual MCQ benchmark that measures how well LLMs understand 
proverbs at two levels:
- Task A (Literal): What does the proverb say word-for-word in English?
- Task B (Cultural): What life lesson or cultural wisdom does it convey?
The "Cultural Reasoning Gap" = performance gap between Task A and Task B.

2. DATASET STATISTICS
- 6 languages: Yoruba (2,000), English (2,278), Arabic (803), French (160), 
  German (141), Spanish (62) = 5,444 proverbs total
- 3 prompting strategies: Zero-Shot (ZS), CoT-English (CoT-EN), CoT-Cross-Lingual (CoT-XL)
- 36 CSV files, ~32,000 MCQ items total
- 22 columns per CSV: task, strategy, language, sample_id, source_proverb, 
  proverb_en, correct_meaning, cultural_context_available, Choice_A-D, Answer, 
  distractor_1-3, raw_response, parse, complete, len_r, distinct, quality

3. GENERATION PIPELINE
- ZS: Cerebras Qwen3-235B, temperature 0.0, batched 5/call
- CoT-EN: Groq LLaMA 3.3-70B, temperature 1.0, 8 parallel workers
- CoT-XL: Groq LLaMA 3.1-8B, temperature 0.4, 8 parallel workers
- Parser: 5-strategy JSON parser (direct, embedded array, quoted strings, 
  numbered list, bullets)
- Quality scoring: parse*40 + complete*30 + distinct*30 (out of 100)
- Answer shuffling: deterministic seed=42 into A-D

5. KNOWN DATA ISSUES
- Yoruba Task A ZS: 368 rows (18.4%) all-NaN (parse failures from Cerebras)
- English Task A ZS: 31 rows all-NaN
- English Task A ZS: 472 duplicate sample_ids out of 2,278
- English Task B ZS: Copies directly from Task A ZS because Cultural_Context == Translation
- CoT-XL files: Widespread NaN rows (119 Yoruba, 194 English, 38 Arabic, 8 French, 5 Spanish, 9 German)
- Task A Arabic ZS: First 3 rows (MID0001-0003) all NaN

6. SHORTCUT AUDIT RESULTS (CRITICAL)
Blind evaluator LLaMA 3.1-8B sees only proverb + 4 choices, not correct answer.
Target: ~25% random. >45% flagged as surface shortcuts.

Task A:
  Yoruba: ~64.6% (+39.6pp) FLAGGED
  German: ~56.0% (+31.0pp) FLAGGED
  Spanish: ~61.3% (+36.3pp) FLAGGED
  French: ~50.0% (+25.0pp) FLAGGED
  English: ~36.8% (+11.8pp) ELEVATED
  Arabic: ~23.4% (-1.6pp) ACCEPTABLE

Task B:
  English: ~89.9% (+64.9pp) SEVERE
  Spanish: ~93.5% (+68.5pp) SEVERE
  German: ~92.2% (+67.2pp) SEVERE
  French: ~82.5% (+57.5pp) SEVERE
  Arabic: ~79.1% (+54.1pp) SEVERE
  Yoruba: ~66.5% (+41.5pp) MODERATE

Authors' framing: Task B high scores for European languages are "EXPECTED" 
because the evaluator knows the cultural meaning from training data. 
Yoruba's lower score is the "Cultural Gap Signal."

7. NOVELTY CLAIMS
(a) Two-task literal/cultural split (no prior work)
(b) Automated LLM-generated distractors at scale with audit protocol
(c) First cultural reasoning evaluation for AfriqueLLM models

10. ETHICAL / LIMITATIONS ACKNOWLEDGED
- Yoruba proverbs from copyrighted collection; release MCQ items only under CC BY-NC 4.0
- French (160), German (141), Spanish (62) lack statistical power
- LLM-generated distractors not validated by human cultural experts
- Single evaluator culture in audit; multi-model committees would be more robust
- Shortcut audit mitigates surface-level biases; deeper cultural biases acknowledged
