# NLP Conference Tiers — CORRECTION

## Your Misconception

You said: *"EACL and TACL are tier B conferences"*

**This is wrong.** Here's the truth:

---

## ACL Conference Hierarchy (All A* / Tier 1)

| Conference | Tier | Type | Acceptance | Prestige |
|------------|------|------|------------|----------|
| **ACL** | A* (Top) | Conference | ~21% | Global flagship |
| **EMNLP** | A* (Top) | Conference | ~26% | Empirical flagship |
| **NAACL** | A* (Top) | Conference | ~24% | North American flagship |
| **EACL** | A* (Top) | Conference | ~25% | **European flagship** |
| **COLING** | A* (Top) | Conference (biennial) | ~25% | International flagship |

**ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, EACL, and COLING are ALL the same tier.** There is no B-tier among them. They are the "Big 5" of NLP.

EACL is NOT lower than ACL. It is the European chapter of the exact same organization (ACL). Papers at EACL are identical in quality to ACL papers. The only difference is geographic rotation:
- ACL: Global (any location)
- NAACL: North America
- EACL: Europe / Africa / Middle East
- AACL: Asia-Pacific

---

## TACL is NOT a Conference — It's a Journal

| Venue | Tier | Type | What It Is |
|-------|------|------|------------|
| **TACL** | A* (Top) | **Journal** | Transactions of the ACL, published by MIT Press |

TACL is the **most prestigious NLP publication venue** outside of conferences. It is:
- A journal (not a conference)
- Published by MIT Press
- Has rolling submissions (no deadline)
- If accepted, presented at the next ACL/EMNLP/NAACL
- Often harder to get into than conferences
- Gets more citations than conference papers

**TACL > ACL in many people's minds.** Journals are permanently archived; conferences are ephemeral.
