================================================================================
1. THREE-COMPONENT BENCHMARK
================================================================================

The project proposes a unified ProverbGap benchmark with THREE evaluation 
formats, all measuring the same "Cultural Reasoning Gap":

A. MCQ (Multiple Choice) — ~32K items, 36 CSVs, COMPLETED but flawed
B. Generation — Free-text explanations, scored with BLEU/ChrF++/BERTScore/COMET
C. Fill-in-the-Blank (Cloze) — Meaning-level cloze, masks words in interpretation

All three use the same 6 languages, same 2-task split (Literal A / Cultural B),
same model set (LLaMA-3.1-8B, LLaMA-3.3-70B, Qwen3-235B, AfriqueLLM variants).

================================================================================
2. MCQ STATUS (COMPLETED BUT CRITICALLY FLAWED)
================================================================================

- 36 CSVs, ~32K items, 2 tasks × 3 strategies × 6 languages
- Generation: Cerebras Qwen3-235B (ZS), Groq LLaMA 3.3-70B (CoT-EN), Groq LLaMA 3.1-8B (CoT-XL)
- SHORTCUT AUDIT FAILURE: Blind LLaMA 3.1-8B evaluator gets 80-93% on Task B 
  (vs 25% random). Task A also flagged at 50-64% for most languages.
- Authors' plan: "Disclose and keep" — frame high scores as "expected memorization"
  for high-resource languages, Yoruba's lower score as "Cultural Gap Signal"
- Known issues: 18.4% NaN rows (Yoruba A ZS), 472 duplicate IDs (English), 
  English Task B ZS = copy of Task A ZS, unvalidated quality metric
