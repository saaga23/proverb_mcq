## 8. ENCODER BASELINES DECISION

**Status:** Enabled in code but expected to score ~0% accuracy.

**Why they score 0%:**
- `sentence-transformers` is broken due to Keras 3 conflict on Kaggle
- Encoder evaluation uses cosine similarity between proverb embedding and option embeddings
- Without sentence-transformers, embeddings cannot be computed → random-guess performance

**Decision: KEEP them running.**
- They are wrapped in `try/except` (line 1486–1494) — failures do NOT crash the pipeline
- 0% is actually informative: it shows that shallow semantic similarity (cosine) does NOT solve proverb comprehension
- This is a valid paper finding: "Encoder baselines fail, confirming that proverb understanding requires reasoning, not surface similarity"
- Do NOT spend time fixing them. The paper narrative benefits from this result.

**If you want to fix them later:** Downgrade `sentence-transformers` to `<3.0` or pin `keras<3.0`.

## 11. HUMAN VALIDATION PROTOCOL (For EACL Competitiveness)

**Required:** 2 annotators per language = 6 total  
**Timeline:** Start within 2 weeks of N=700 completion  
**Duration:** ~2–3 hours per annotator (sample of 50 items per language = 150 total)

### What to validate:
| Criterion | Question | Acceptable Threshold |
|-----------|----------|---------------------|
| Correctness | Is the correct answer truly correct? | ≥95% agreement |
| Distractor quality | Are distractors plausible but wrong? | ≥80% "plausible" rating |
| Cultural appropriateness | Is the content culturally sensitive? | 0 critical issues |
| Clarity | Is the question unambiguous? | ≥90% "clear" rating |

### Process:
1. Sample 50 S2 items per language (stratified: 20 easy, 20 medium, 10 hard by item difficulty from fig8)
2. Each annotator independently rates on 4 criteria above
3. Aggregate: majority vote for correctness, average for quality ratings
4. Flag items with inter-annotator disagreement for manual review
5. Report Cohen's Kappa between annotators as reliability metric

### Why this matters:
- EACL reviewers expect human validation for new benchmarks
- Kinayat et al. (EACL 2026) had 3 annotators with κ=0.82
- Our target: κ ≥ 0.75 between annotator pairs
