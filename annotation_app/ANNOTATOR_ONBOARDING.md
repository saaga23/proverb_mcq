# Annotator Onboarding — ProverbGap Human Validation

Thank you for participating in this study. This guide will get you started in under 5 minutes.

## What you will do

You will read proverbs from **English**, **Arabic**, or **Yoruba** and select the option that best matches the proverb's meaning. There are **62 items** total, including a few attention checks. The task takes approximately **15–20 minutes**.

## Before you start

- Use a **desktop or laptop** browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Edge). Mobile is not supported.
- Find a **quiet environment** where you can concentrate.
- Have your **consent form** ready if you need to sign it separately.
- If you are annotating **Arabic** or **Yoruba**, make sure you are comfortable reading those languages.

## Step-by-step

1. Open the annotation app: **https://annotationapp.vercel.app**
2. Read the study information and confirm consent.
3. Fill in the short demographic form:
   - Native language(s)
   - Consent confirmation
   - Annotator name or ID
   - Age range
   - Education level
   - Country of residence
   - Agreement to the data-use statement
4. Click **Start Annotating**.
5. For each item:
   - Read the proverb.
   - Read the four options (A, B, C, D).
   - Select the option that best captures the proverb's meaning.
   - Choose your confidence level: **High**, **Medium**, or **Low**.
   - Click **Submit & Next**.
6. You may pause and resume later — your progress is saved automatically.
7. When you finish all items, you will see a completion screen.

## Tips for high-quality annotations

- **Avoid literal word-for-word translations** if they sound unnatural in English. Choose the option that best expresses the proverb's intended meaning.
- **Trust your first instinct** for most items, but use the pause button if you need more time.
- **Watch for attention checks**: some items explicitly tell you which option to choose. These are used to verify that you are reading carefully.
- **Do not use external tools** (dictionaries, translators, search engines) during annotation. This study measures native-speaker intuition.

## Data and privacy

- Your annotations are stored anonymously in a secure database.
- The demographic form does not collect personally identifiable information beyond what is necessary for analysis.
- Results will be reported in aggregate. No individual responses will be identified in any publication.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Page will not load | Refresh the page or try a different browser. |
| Options look broken or missing | Refresh the page. If the problem persists, contact the study coordinator. |
| Accidentally selected the wrong answer | Click a different option before submitting. |
| Need to stop mid-task | Your progress is saved automatically. Return later with the same browser. |
